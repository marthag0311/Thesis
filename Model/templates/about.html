SOLARWINDS

Your go-to platform for simulating and predicting renewable and non-renewable energy capacities.

The Mission

The mission is for countries to use the platform to explore renewable energy capacity deployment strategies.

Vision

The visions is for stakeholders to find the platform credible.

Values

  • Facilitate decision-making.
  • Exploration of strategies

Benefits of Using Our Platform

By utilizing our platform, the following are the benefits you gain:

  • Enhanced decision-making: The model facilitates informed decision-making by allowing stakeholders to explore various strategies for renewable energy expansion. By simulating different strategies, stakeholders can identify the most effective approaches for achieving renewable energy capacity target and minimizing reliance on non-renewable sources. In addition, the platform’s ability to visualize complex data through histograms and line plots enhances user’s comprehension. The line plots and histograms have annotations that annotates when the pathways reach the targets, thus improving the overall decision-making process.
  • Informed strategy development: The platform provides a probabilistic view of potential outcomes, offering insights into the variability and uncertainty of energy capacity transitions. This allows stakeholders to understand the range of possible future pathways and develop strategies that are robust under various conditions.

Get Started Today

Whether you are an energy planner, policymaker, or a scholar, the platform helps energy planners by aiding in strategic planning and investment decisions as energy planners utilize the platform for potential growth pathways of renewable energy capacity. The platform helps policymakers to inform policy and regulation decisions, considering the impact on renewable energy adoption, as policymakers may leverage the insights provided by the simulations. The platform also helps scholars for academic and research purposes through exploring various strategies and analysing the effectiveness of different strategies in promoting renewable energy deployment.
