Welcome to our energy capacity analysis tool

This application allows you to simulate and visualize the growth of renewable energy capacity while decreasing non-renewable energy capacity over time. By inputting your planned annual renewable addition, initial values and target capacities, you can see projections of future energy capacities, along with percentile distributions to help understand the potential variability in outcomes. Use this tool to make informed decisions about energy planning and to explore different scenarios for achieving your energy goals.

Energy Capacity Simulations

Energy Capacity Simulations, which are cumulative results or pathways, are visualized to understand the distribution and the outcomes effectively. The plots for renewable and non-renewable capacities use different colours to differentiate between the results that meet the target, which are in green and those that do not meet the target, which are in orange. This highlights the pathways that achieve the desired target, allowing for an immediate visual assessment of success rates.
